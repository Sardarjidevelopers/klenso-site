What it finds

Large files and real duplicates

Duplicates are checked by content, not by name. Klenso never removes the last copy.

Old project junk

Forgotten node_modules, build folders and virtual environments you haven't touched in 90 days. They rebuild themselves.

Developer caches

Gradle, npm, pip and pub caches, and your Temp folder. Often several gigabytes, and they refill on their own.

What Klenso will and won't touch

  • It only looks in your own folders (Downloads, Desktop, Documents, Videos, Pictures, Music) and a fixed list of cache folders, plus project folders under your user profile.
  • Your own files go to the Recycle Bin, so you can undo. Only folders that rebuild themselves are cleared for good, and Klenso labels them.
  • It never enters Windows, Program Files, AppData (apart from the named caches), .git, .ssh or cloud-only OneDrive files.
  • Signing keys, certificates and password databases (.jks, .pem, .pfx, .kdbx and similar) are never offered for removal.
  • Every item is re-checked right before it's removed. Anything that changed since the scan is skipped.
  • No registry cleaning, no "speed boosters", no scare screens.

Klenso is careful, but no tool can promise it will never remove something you wanted. Keep backups of anything important.

Questions

Can I try it first?

Yes. The scan is free and shows exactly what Klenso could clean. Cleaning needs a licence.

Do you offer refunds?

We don't refund for change of mind, which is why the scan is free. If Klenso doesn't work as described, your rights under Australian Consumer Law apply and we'll sort it out. See the terms.

How many PCs can I use it on?

Up to 3 of your own devices per licence. Moving to a new PC? Email support and we'll reset it.

Windows says the app is unrecognised.

Early releases aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each download page shows its SHA-256 hash so you can check the file.

Is it cracked-proof?

No program running on your own PC can be. Licences are signed, tied to your email and revocable, which is enough to keep honest people honest.
