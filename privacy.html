Privacy

Draft written for launch. Not legal advice: have it reviewed before taking real payments. Last updated 16 November 2026.

The short version

Klenso runs on your PC. It doesn't collect usage data, send crash reports, or upload file names, paths or file contents. We can't see what is on your drive.

What leaves your PC, and when

  • Activation (once per device): when you enter a licence key, Klenso sends the key and a random device ID to our activation server. The random ID is made on your PC and identifies nothing about you or your hardware. The server remembers which random IDs have used which key, so it can enforce the 3-device limit. After activation, licence checks happen offline.
  • Check for updates (only when you click it): Klenso downloads one small file from this site (version number and a list of withdrawn key IDs). Nothing about you is sent beyond what any web request includes, such as your IP address, which this site's host may log.

Buying

Payments are processed by Paddle, which is the seller of record. Paddle collects your payment details, email and billing country under its own privacy policy. We receive the email address you used so we can send your licence key. We keep a record of each key issued (key ID, email, date) so we can support you and revoke leaked keys. We don't see your card details.

Email

We'll email you your licence key, support replies, and a notice if a paid major upgrade is released. We won't sell or share your email.

Your choices

Email support@sardarjidevelopers.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted (we may need to keep a minimal record of the sale for tax and licensing).
